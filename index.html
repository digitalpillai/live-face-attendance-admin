Add leave or permission for a staff member

For when someone cannot ask in the app, for example when they phone in sick. It is saved as approved at once. Leave: up to 31 days in one go, up to 3 months back. Permission: up to 4 hours on one day.

Leave and permission

Every request that touches the chosen month. Approved leave shows in the monthly register (CL, SL, EL, CO = paid leave; LOP = loss of pay) and on the live attendance page. Cancel takes it out again.

HR logins use this dashboard, not the phone app. For the showrooms ticked here they can add and edit staff, make staff inactive, reset faces and passwords, upload staff from Excel, and see attendance and reports. Face match minimum

Higher is stricter. 0.40 means a 40% match. If genuine staff are often refused, lower it a little. If a wrong person ever gets through, raise it.

Working hours (default for all showrooms)

Late = checked in more than the grace time after opening. Left early = checked out more than the grace time before closing. A showroom can have its own hours (Showrooms tab, Edit).

Check-in reminder on the phone

The phone rings like an alarm (app 1.3.0 or newer) on working days when the person has not checked in: not on their weekly off, holidays, approved leave, or during an approved permission. The reminders stop as soon as they check in.
